The Threat

Fiber Drones Don’t Play by the Old Rules.

Modern attack drones increasingly fly on fiber-optic tethers instead of radio links, making them immune to traditional RF jamming and electronic warfare. They are cheap, precise, and are being used to hunt individual soldiers on the modern battlefield.

Vehicle-mounted and static counter-drone systems can’t follow a soldier on foot into a trench, a treeline, or an urban breach. The threat moves with the soldier — so the defense has to move with the soldier, too.

ASPIS is built to close that gap: a self-contained, backpack-mounted shield that goes wherever the mission goes.

Capabilities

One System. Four Layers of Defense.

ASPIS runs a continuous detect–decide–defeat cycle, escalating its response to match the threat.

01 — Sense

Detect

Continuously scans the surrounding airspace to acquire and track approaching drones, day or night.

02 — Disrupt

Jam

Disrupts the drone’s control and video links, breaking its ability to navigate or transmit.

03 — Defeat

Neutralize

Escalates to disable the threat outright when jamming alone isn’t enough to stop it.

04 — Disable

Compromise

Blinds the drone by knocking out its camera feed, and grounds it by disabling its propellers mid-flight.

How It Works

From Threat to Shield in Seconds.

Wear It

ASPIS integrates into a standard backpack form factor — no extra bulk, no separate carry.

Scan

The system continuously monitors the surrounding airspace for drone signatures.

Engage

On detection, ASPIS automatically jams, neutralizes, or compromises the threat.

Protect

The soldier keeps moving, hands free, shielded by a system that never stops watching.
